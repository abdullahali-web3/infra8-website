# Infra8 — Project Brief (final agenda, extracted from the positioning chat)

Source: the B2B positioning + homepage-copy conversation. Where the chat revised itself, the **later** version wins. Items in [brackets] are unconfirmed facts the client must supply. Do not publish a number, logo, certification or claim that can't be backed up.

## 1. Business context
- Client: Infra8, a new engineering agency. Assumed audience: funded startups and SMBs in Western markets. Assumed: few or no public case studies yet. (Confirm both.)
- Two buyers per deal: economic buyer (founder/CEO: speed, price clarity, low risk) and technical evaluator (CTO/eng lead: won't create a mess they inherit).
- Stage: new, so sell trust and risk reduction, not scale proof. Substitute proof until case studies exist: sample estimate, sample architecture diagram, sample audit report, engineer bios.
- 12–24 month goal: move one-off projects into retainers (managed DevOps, dedicated squads). Plant "we build it, then run it" language now.
- Market: red ocean (freelancers, dev shops, DevOps consultancies, marketplaces like Upwork/Toptal). Lead with differentiation, not education. Transparent pricing is the wedge (most competitors hide it).
- Sales motion: sales-led with a low-friction entry. The 24-hour estimate is the "self-serve" moment; everything funnels there.

## 2. Positioning
- Core idea: **"Build it and run it."** One senior team that builds and operates the product. Avoids the handoff gap (dev shop ships, infra breaks).
- AI-native workflows are *how we deliver*, not a third service. No "AI agency" hype.
- Two services only: **Build** (0→1 product dev, dedicated squad) and **Run** (cloud, DevOps, security). "Cyber" is NOT a separate service; security lives inside Run.

## 3. Homepage structure (final)
Nav: Services · How we work · Pricing · Work · Company | **Get an estimate** (no "Products", no "Contact us").

1. **Hero.** Eyebrow: "Senior engineering team for startups and scaling products". H1: "We build MVPs for founders. We run the cloud for live products." Two equal CTAs: "I'm building an MVP: Get MVP estimate in 24 hrs" and "I run a live product: Get a free infra audit". Microcopy: Fixed scope · You own all code · NDA on request. Replace the tech-icon grid with a mini estimate card or a Launch/Build/Scale visual.
2. **Trust strip.** Real client logos ("Teams we've built and run infrastructure for") OR, if none, a stack strip: AWS · Google Cloud · Azure · Terraform · Kubernetes · Docker. No fake logos. Badges only if certifications are held.
3. **Stage cards** ("Engineering for every stage of your product"): LAUNCH (idea/pre-seed, MVP, from $[10k], [6–10] wks), BUILD (live MVP, dedicated squad, from $[8k]/mo), SCALE (growth-stage, cloud/DevOps/security, retainers from $[4k]/mo, start with the free audit). Each card has a price/timeline line and a specific secondary link ("See how MVP builds work", "See squad plans", "What the audit covers").
4. **Four commitments on every project** (REPLACES the old "problem" section, which was cut as generic): you own everything; named senior engineers; written scope and price before we start; visible progress (weekly demos for MVPs, monthly uptime/cost/risk report for infra). Optional "what we don't do" line under each (e.g. "No lock-in").
5. **How it works**, two tracks. MVP: send idea → estimate in 24h → discovery sprint (1–2 wks) → build with weekly demos → launch and handover. Infra: free audit → findings report → fix → managed retainer.
6. **AI-native workflow** ("Faster delivery. Senior engineers stay in charge."): AI-assisted code review on every PR, automated test generation, infra scans, faster scoping. No "% faster" claim unless measured.
7. **Tool stack** (NEW, after AI workflow, before pricing): two tabs. MVP/product teams: React, Next.js, React Native, Flutter, Node.js, Python (FastAPI/Django), PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, AI model APIs, Figma, GitHub, Vercel, AWS. Live products at scale: AWS/GCP/Azure, Terraform/Pulumi, Docker/Kubernetes, GitHub Actions/GitLab CI/ArgoCD, Grafana/Prometheus/Datadog/Sentry, Vault/Snyk/Trivy/GuardDuty. Keep "why this stack" lines (standard tools, your accounts, no lock-in). List only what the team has really shipped with; 6–8 items per group; no implied partner status. Closer: "Use a different stack? We'll work in yours."
8. **Pricing snapshot** ("See the price range before you talk to us"): discovery sprint $2k–5k; lean MVP $10k–25k; full MVP $25k–60k; dedicated squad $8k–20k/mo; infra audit free review then $3k–8k deep audit; managed DevOps $4k–20k/mo. Note: price depends on scope, integrations, compliance, uptime; the estimate gives the exact number. UNVALIDATED: check against 5–10 competitor quotes and real delivery cost/margin. (An earlier chat table had different ranges; the later table above is the current one.)
9. **Proof**: sample MVP scope doc, sample architecture diagram, sample anonymized audit report (downloads), engineer bios with real experience and LinkedIn; later 2–3 case study cards with a real number.
10. **Who it's for / not for**: fit = defined-idea founders with budget, live MVP needing weekly shipping, real-user product needing accountable cloud/uptime/security. Not a fit = cheapest possible build, 24/7 NOC with [15-min] response, no defined user/problem yet.
11. **FAQ**: code/infra ownership, non-technical founders, how a 24h estimate works, cloud access (least privilege, revocable, logged), who writes the code [confirm], stopping terms [confirm], time zones [confirm], SOC 2/ISO 27001 (preparation only; accredited auditor certifies).
12. **Final CTA**: "Tell us where your product is. You'll hear back in 24 hours." Both CTAs + microcopy.
Footer: Product Development · Cloud & DevOps · Pricing · How we work · Company · [Email] · "Response within 24 hours on business days".

Landing pages to follow the homepage: MVP development, dedicated squad, infra audit, managed DevOps. Buyer-specific problem framing lives there, not on the homepage.

## 6. Open questions for the client (blockers for copy and schema)
1. Production domain, legal entity name, founding year, target markets and time zones.
2. Are there real client logos or case studies? Any certifications or cloud partner status?
3. Who are the named engineers (names, roles, years, past stack, LinkedIn)? Is "our own senior engineers write the code" true?
4. Confirm pricing ranges, minimum retainer term (chat suggests month-to-month after [3] months), and response-time guarantees.
5. Free audit scope (suggested: 45-minute call + one-page written summary).
6. Which stack has the team actually shipped with (drives the stack section)?
7. Has the competitor audit (8–10 competitors: hero claim, CTA, pricing transparency, proof) been done? Ideal first client profile in one sentence?
8. Estimate form: what does a client need to provide so the estimate is truly deliverable in 24h?
9. Brand: logo, colors, fonts, tone, any existing Figma/design files.
